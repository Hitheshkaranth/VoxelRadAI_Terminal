# MedGemma benchmark

Runs the app's exact analysis pipeline (same prompts, parser, and lung-pattern pass) over public, labelled chest radiographs and scores it.

```sh
cd benchmark
python3 fetch.py                                   # downloads a stratified sample → img/, manifest.json
node --experimental-strip-types run.mts            # ~1 min/image; resumable → results.jsonl
python3 score.py final results.jsonl               # per-class sensitivity, normal specificity, FP types, latency
python3 fuse.py                                    # classifier thresholds on ChestX-Det dev → app/server/thresholds.json
python3 -m unittest test_score.py                  # scorer / fusion unit tests (no model)
node --experimental-strip-types rescore.mts        # re-parse stored raw outputs after parser changes
```
Requires LM Studio serving `medgemma-27b-it` on `localhost:1234`.

Committed alongside the scripts: `manifest.json` (the exact 56 images and their labels) and `results.jsonl` (every raw model output, parsed findings and lung-pattern grade from the Sep 2026 run), so the numbers below can be audited or re-scored without re-running the model: `python3 score.py final results.jsonl`. Images are not committed — `fetch.py` re-downloads them.

## Methodology

### Datasets
| Set | Films | Labels | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Original (`manifest.json`) | 56: 44 NIH ChestX-ray14 + 12 Kermany pediatric | NIH labels are NLP-mined from reports (~90% accurate); no boxes | All results below dated Sep 2026. **In-sample**: prompts, the classifier type map and the fusion rule were chosen while looking at these films |
| ChestX-Det (`manifest-cxd.json`, `python3 fetch.py chestxdet`) | 217 NIH films re-annotated by 3 radiologists (13 classes with boxes, Apache-2.0): 60 normals, then films added until every class has ≥ 20 | Radiologist labels and boxes | **dev** 106 films for tuning, **test** 111 films for reporting. The split is a hash of the image id, fixed in the manifest |

- NIH ChestX-ray14 (test split, via Hugging Face `BahaaEldin0/NIH-Chest-Xray-14`): 12 *No Finding* plus effusion, cardiomegaly, pneumothorax, edema, consolidation, pneumonia, infiltration, atelectasis, mass and nodule. With 4–6 films per class, per-class numbers are directional.
- Kermany pediatric pneumonia (`hf-vision/chest-xray-pneumonia`, test split): 6 normal and 6 pneumonia.
- ChestX-Det (`MedOtter/ChestX-Det`, official test split). Classes: consolidation, effusion, nodule, mass, diffuse nodule, atelectasis, pneumothorax, cardiomegaly, fibrosis, pleural thickening, fracture, calcification and emphysema. There is no edema class. Test split: 29 normals, 48 consolidation, 43 effusion, 19 nodule, 9 mass, 12 cardiomegaly, 11 atelectasis, 10 pneumothorax (a film can carry several labels).

### What is tuned on what
| Choice | Tuned on | Reported on |
|---|---|---|
| TorchXRayVision per-class thresholds and calibration knots (`app/server/thresholds.json`) | ChestX-Det **dev** (`fuse.py`, stage A) | ChestX-Det **test** (below) |
| Fusion rule: keep MedGemma's findings, add MAIRA-2 effusion / nodule-mass / atelectasis findings the classifier supports | Proposed on the 56-image set; variant chosen on ChestX-Det **dev** | ChestX-Det **test** (below) |
| Prompts, parser, finding-type regexes | 56-image set, so in-sample | ChestX-Det test (below), prompt hash `12ccde05461e` |
| App reliability table (`app/src/lib/reliability.generated.ts`) | — | MedGemma on ChestX-Det **test**, generated by `score.py --emit-ts` |

No number is edited by hand. `score.py --emit-ts` writes the app's table and `fuse.py` writes the thresholds.

### Statistics
- Every rate has a **Wilson 95% interval**, `k/n (p%, CI lo–hi)`. With n under about 20 the intervals are wide; read them before comparing two models.
- **Sensitivity** per label class: a film counts as detected when any abnormal finding's text matches that class.
- **Specificity**: normal films with no abnormal finding.
- **Precision** is reported two ways:
  - *image-level*: one count per film per reported type. This is what the app shows.
  - *finding-level*: every finding counts, so a bilateral effusion counts twice. Findings tagged `source: maira2` by the fusion are a separate group.
- **Run-to-run variance**: outputs vary between runs at temperature 0.2. `RUNS=3 ./run_all.sh` writes `results-cxd-r1..3.jsonl`, and `score.py final r1 r2 r3` prints mean [min–max] across runs. The Sep 2026 numbers are a single run.
- **Localisation**: a detection counts as localised when the centre of a predicted box of that finding lies inside a radiologist box of the same class. Boxes thinner than 1% of the image in either dimension are ignored. MAIRA-2 outputs that lost their `<obj>` tags (`parse: "fallback"`) have no boxes. They are left out of the localisation denominator, and the count left out is printed.

### Classifier thresholds (ChestX-Det, Oct 2026)
`python3 fuse.py` runs stage A from the committed `txrv-cxd.jsonl`, which holds TorchXRayVision probabilities for all 217 films. No model is needed.

For each cross-check kind, the probability is the maximum over the kind's pathology outputs, as in the app. On dev, the threshold maximising Youden J is chosen from a 0.05–0.95 grid. Kinds with fewer than 10 dev positives keep the models' 0.5 operating point; for edema, ChestX-Det has no label at all. The test results below are held out. Negatives are all other test films, including films with other abnormalities.

| Kind | Thr (dev) | Dev positives | Test AUC | Test sensitivity @thr | Test specificity @thr | Sensitivity @0.5 | Specificity @0.5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pneumothorax | 0.39 | 10 | 0.76 | 7/10 (CI 40–89) | 69/101 (CI 59–77) | 2/10 (CI 6–51) | 91/101 (CI 83–95) |
| effusion | 0.50 | 34 | 0.83 | 32/43 (CI 60–85) | 55/68 (CI 70–88) | same | same |
| cardiomegaly | 0.63 | 15 | 0.85 | 8/12 (CI 39–86) | 86/99 (CI 79–92) | 11/12 (CI 65–99) | 51/99 (CI 42–61) |
| nodule / mass | 0.53 | 28 | 0.55 | 13/27 (CI 31–66) | 56/84 (CI 56–76) | 21/27 (CI 59–89) | 22/84 (CI 18–36) |
| atelectasis | 0.54 | 10 | 0.63 | 6/11 (CI 28–79) | 51/100 (CI 41–61) | 7/11 (CI 35–85) | 47/100 (CI 38–57) |
| opacity (← consolidation) | 0.53 | 47 | 0.88 | 46/48 (CI 86–99) | 33/63 (CI 40–64) | 47/48 (CI 89–100) | 29/63 (CI 34–58) |
| edema | 0.50 (default) | 0 | — | — | 64/111 | — | 64/111 |

What this means for the app:
- The classifier is a reasonable check for opacity, cardiomegaly and effusion.
- At 0.5 it suppresses most true pneumothoraces. The tuned threshold of 0.39 trades about 22 points of specificity for 50 points of sensitivity, on 10 positives.
- For nodule/mass (AUC 0.55) and atelectasis (AUC 0.63) the classifier barely separates positives from negatives. A "not supported" verdict for those kinds carries little evidence, and gating MAIRA-2's nodule and atelectasis additions on it is weak.

Calibration knots map a classifier probability to the fraction of dev films positive for that class, using pooled-adjacent-violators with at most 8 knots, interpolated linearly. ChestX-Det is enriched (about 20 films per class), so these are probabilities **in this sample**, not clinical prevalence. Use them for display only.

### Running the GPU benchmarks (one model at a time)
The machine has 24 GB of unified memory. MedGemma 27B needs about 16 GB and MAIRA-2 in bf16 needs about 14 GB, so they cannot be resident together, and nothing else may be loaded in LM Studio during a run. `run_all.sh` runs the steps in sequence:
1. Refuses to start while LM Studio has another model loaded. Pass `--force-unload` to override; it never unloads silently.
2. Loads `$MODEL` with a 16k context and runs `run.mts` `RUNS` times.
3. Unloads everything and waits until `lms ps` shows nothing loaded.
4. Runs `run_maira2.py` on MPS.
5. Runs the CPU steps: `fuse.py`, then `score.py --split test` for each file, all saved to `scores-cxd.txt`, then regenerates the app's reliability table from the held-out test split.

`run.mts` checks `/v1/models` before the loop and stops if `$MODEL` is not served. Each row records `model`, `temp`, `finish` and `promptHash`, a sha1 of the system, analysis and lung-pattern prompts, so results from different prompt versions are never pooled by mistake.

| Variant | Dev | Test |

What this changed:
- **The classifier no longer removes MedGemma findings.** Filtering cost 17 true abnormal films on test for 3 more normals called normal. Unsupported findings stay in the draft, marked *unconfirmed* (dashed outline, worded as possible in the report, listed on the pre-sign checklist). `fuse.py --filter-medgemma` reproduces the old behaviour.
- **MAIRA-2 as a second reader is the one change that helps**: effusion 30% → 53% with no change in normals, when its findings are added only if the classifier supports them. The app's optional second reader applies the same gate.
- MedGemma's weak spots on radiologist labels: pneumothorax is over-called (10 of 36 films with a pneumothorax call were right), effusion is under-called, fractures are missed (0/12), and its boxes rarely land on the lesion (16%). The app's reliability table now shows these test-split numbers.

## MAIRA-2 comparison — Sep 2026, same 56 images
```sh
# needs transformers 4.46–4.51 (MAIRA-2's remote code), torch, pillow, protobuf, sentencepiece, accelerate
# and the weights (license accepted at huggingface.co/microsoft/maira-2); MAIRA_PATH defaults to ~/.cache/maira-2
python run_maira2.py                               # grounded reporting, bf16 on MPS, ~8 s/image → results-maira2.jsonl
python3 run_maira2.py --rescore results-maira2.jsonl   # re-classify stored sentences after rule changes (no model)
python3 score.py final results-maira2.jsonl
```
MAIRA-2 generates a MIMIC-style *Findings* section (technique = view, no indication or prior), one box per sentence. Each sentence becomes a finding; a rule-based classifier marks negated sentences ("normal without effusion", "No pneumothorax") and well-positioned devices as normal. All findings are severity `minor` because the model gives no severity or confidence. In 5/56 outputs the model dropped its `<obj>` tags mid-report, which breaks its own parser; those are recovered as plain sentences without boxes (`parse: "fallback"`).

Synonyms added to `score.py` for MAIRA-2's wording (*opacification*, *alveolar*, *round opacity*) leave MedGemma's scores unchanged.

|---|---|---|

| Right finding type (all labels) | 25/49 | 19/49 |

- Pediatric films are outside MAIRA-2's training data (adult MIMIC-CXR): all 6 pneumonias were "abnormal" but described as cardiomegaly/vascular congestion, and one as large bilateral pneumothoraces with effusions.

## TorchXRayVision cross-check — Sep 2026
```sh
python run_txrv.py                                          # probabilities per image → txrv.jsonl (uses app/server/txrv_server.py)
python3 run_txrv.py --filter results.jsonl results-txrv.jsonl   # drop findings the classifier does not support
python3 score.py final results-txrv.jsonl
```
Models: `densenet121-res224-chex`, `-mimic_ch`, `-pc` averaged — deliberately not the default `all` model, which includes NIH ChestX-ray14 in training. A finding's type is taken from its title (description only if the title matches no type) and is unsupported when the probability for that type is < 0.5 (each model's operating point; not tuned on this set). The app now uses per-class thresholds tuned on ChestX-Det dev instead (see *Classifier thresholds* above); the table below is the 0.5 rule.

| | MedGemma | + cross-check |
|---|---|---|
| Adult normals called normal | 8/12 | 9/12 |
| Adult abnormal flagged | 22/32 | 21/32 (the dropped film's only finding was a false pneumothorax) |
| Right finding type | 25/49 | 25/49 |
| Findings removed | — | 4, all false |

Classifier alone, AUC vs normals (n = 2–10 per label, directional): pneumothorax 1.00, consolidation 0.96, edema 0.94, cardiomegaly 0.93, mass 0.92, nodule 0.90, any abnormality 0.87, pneumonia 0.82, atelectasis 0.82, effusion 0.76 (two labelled effusions scored 0.08 and 0.10).

Tried and rejected: adding effusions the classifier sees but MedGemma missed (at a threshold catching 4/6 effusions it also fired on 20/32 other abnormal films), and a cardiothoracic ratio from the ChestX-Det heart/lung masks (labelled cardiomegaly 0.46–0.60 vs 3/18 normals above 0.5). An early version of the type mapping let description words override the title (e.g. "…or atelectasis" in a consolidation's description) and appeared to remove 5 false findings; fixed before the numbers above.

## What changed in the app because of this
- Devices in a satisfactory position are classified as normal (malpositioned devices keep their severity).
- Each finding card shows the benchmark confirmation rate for its finding type, with a warning when the rate's Wilson lower bound is under 50%, and the model's confidence is labelled "Model-stated". The table is generated: `python3 score.py final <results> [--split test] --emit-ts ../app/src/lib/reliability.generated.ts --label "…"`; `app/src/lib/reliability.ts` only loads it.

## Tried and rejected (measured on subsets)
- **Temperature 0**: same false-positive rate, more findings per false alarm.
- **Two-step "plain report → JSON"**: caught nodules/masses (2/2) and had fewer false alarms, but missed both pneumothoraces and was ~2× slower.
- **Zone re-check of each opacity** (neutral 0–3 grade): graded every region 0, removing true findings along with false ones.
- **Leading yes/no questions**: answer "present" regardless of the image.
- **Checklist of subtle patterns in the main prompt**: induced false findings on normal films.
