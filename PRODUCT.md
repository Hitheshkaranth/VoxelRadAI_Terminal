# RadGenis

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
Radiologists, educators and researchers review X-ray, CT and MRI images, inspect
model evidence, review candidate findings and prepare reports. This is research
and review-support software, not a clinically validated diagnostic device.

## Existing identity
Preserve the existing logo, self-hosted fonts, neutral workspace surfaces,
light/dark themes and image-first layout. Existing tokens and components define
the visual system. The public landing page has its own documented brand treatment.

## Design principles
- Keep the source image and its evidence easy to inspect.
- Distinguish model candidates from reviewed findings.
- Keep controls restrained and their behavior predictable.
- Preserve the reader's view when opening and closing supporting panels.
- Adapt layout structurally to the available browser viewport.

## Accessibility and inclusion
Retain keyboard access, visible focus, modal focus trapping, reduced-motion
support, readable contrast and touch-sized controls. Full accessibility and
physical-device validation remain outstanding, as documented in README.md.
