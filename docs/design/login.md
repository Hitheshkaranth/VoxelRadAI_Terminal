# Login surface

Mode: Operate. Existing Figtree, VoxelRad AI mark and light/dark themes are preserved.

The public CT teaching volume is the visual anchor: a large black cine panel sits beside a solid, quiet sign-in surface. The form is the primary action. The scan traverses the native slices forward and backward; no synthetic anatomy or model findings are shown.

Desktop uses a 1.2:1 split with a restrained 390px form. Mobile reduces the scan to a 270px header, retains playback control and puts the form immediately below. Fields are 54px high, explicit labels remain visible, and organization sign-in is an outlined secondary action. Motion preferences and manual pause take precedence over autoplay.

The direction was adapted from the editorial greeting app sheet: preserve the typeset reading-room greeting and tonal surfaces; omit its dashboard metrics/grid because the sole task here is authentication. No invented counts, credentials or security guarantees are displayed.

Verification: production build and browser checks are recorded with the implementation outcome. Video provenance and reproduction instructions live in `app/public/login/README.md`.
