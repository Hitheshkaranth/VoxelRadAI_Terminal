Privacy

Last updated 15 November 2026

VoxelRad AI is a research and evaluation workspace for radiology images. This page describes what the hosted service at voxelradai.com stores and why. It covers the website and the hosted workspace. If your organization runs its own copy, that organization decides how its data is handled.

Do not upload identifiable patient data unless your organization has authorized it and you have a lawful basis to do so. Prefer de-identified or public images. The hosted service is not a clinical system.

Visiting the website

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to count page views, see which sections are used (for example, which model build is downloaded) and measure loading speed. They do not use cookies. Page addresses are sent without their query string or # fragment. We see only aggregated figures, not individual profiles.

Using the workspace

Accounts are created by a workspace administrator. When you use the workspace we store:

  • Account: your email address and a hashed password, or the identity returned by your organization's single sign-on if that is enabled.
  • Session: an HttpOnly cookie that keeps you signed in. Idle sessions expire automatically.
  • Sign-in protection: recent sign-in attempts and the requesting IP address, used to limit password guessing. These records expire.
  • Studies: the images and volumes you upload, the patient and clinical details you enter, model findings, your review decisions and report drafts.
  • Sharing: share links you create. Anyone holding a link can read its snapshot until it expires or is revoked.
  • Audit log: a record of actions such as sign-ins, study changes and shares, kept for security and accountability.

Model analysis

Images are sent for analysis only when you start an analysis, and only to the model server your workspace is configured to use. Hosted GPU inference is disabled by default; many installations run the model on their own hardware.

Where data is kept

The hosted service runs on Vercel (application functions in the Singapore region) with a Neon PostgreSQL database. Data is sent over HTTPS. We do not sell data, show advertising or use your studies to train models.

Retention and deletion

Expired sessions, sign-in attempts, recovery codes and abandoned uploads are cleaned up automatically. Studies and accounts are kept until they are deleted. There is not yet a fixed retention period for them. To have your account or studies deleted, ask your workspace administrator or contact us.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub. Please do not include patient information in an issue.
