Privacy

Last updated 28 November 2026 (optional model-improvement sharing)

RadGenis is a research and evaluation workspace for radiology images. This page describes what the hosted service at radgenis.com stores and why. It covers the website and the hosted workspace. If your organization runs its own copy, that organization decides how its data is handled.

Do not upload identifiable patient data unless your organization has authorized it and you have a lawful basis to do so. Prefer de-identified or public images. The hosted service is not a clinical system.

Visiting the website

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to count page views, see which sections are used (for example, which model build is downloaded) and measure loading speed. They do not use cookies. Page addresses are sent without their query string or # fragment. We see only aggregated figures, not individual profiles.

Requesting access

If you create an account, we store your name, email address, organization, role and a salted hash of your password (never the password itself). Signing up with Google instead shares your Google account ID, name and verified email address with us; we never receive your Google password. We email you a link to confirm your address and again when an administrator approves the account. Unconfirmed email sign-ups are deleted after 7 days.

If you use the request form, we store your name, email address, organization, role and what you would use the workspace for. We use them only to review and answer your request. Requests are deleted 180 days after they are reviewed. The form also has a hidden field that catches automated spam; it records nothing about you.

Using the workspace

Accounts are created by a workspace administrator. When you use the workspace we store:

  • Account: your email address and a hashed password, or the identity returned by your organization's single sign-on if that is enabled.
  • Session: an HttpOnly cookie that keeps you signed in. Idle sessions expire automatically.
  • Sign-in protection: recent sign-in attempts and the requesting IP address, used to limit password guessing. These records expire.
  • Studies: the images and volumes you upload, the patient and clinical details you enter, model findings, your review decisions and report drafts.
  • Patient register: each patient you register gets a RadGenis ID, stored with the name, sex, date of birth, phone and ABHA number or address you enter. If you enter an Aadhaar number, it is used only to find or match that patient: RadGenis stores a keyed one-way code made from it and its last four digits, never the number itself. Searches by Aadhaar or ABHA are recorded in your account’s audit log. Only you can see the patients you register.
  • Saved volumes: CT and MRI volumes you choose to save to your account (Saved volumes), with the modality, body region, sequence and contrast you enter. They are stored as files in a private Google Cloud Storage bucket in Mumbai, India, and downloaded only through short-lived links issued to you.
  • Sharing: share links you create. Anyone holding a link can read its snapshot until it expires or is revoked.
  • Audit log: a record of actions such as sign-ins, study changes and shares, kept for security and accountability.

Model analysis

Images are sent for analysis only when you start an analysis, and only to the model server your workspace is configured to use. Hosted GPU inference is disabled by default; many installations run the model on their own hardware.

Where data is kept

The hosted service runs on Vercel (application functions in the Singapore region) with a Neon PostgreSQL database. Saved volumes are kept in Google Cloud Storage in Mumbai, India (asia-south1), encrypted at rest. Data is sent over HTTPS. We do not sell data or show advertising.

Model improvement (optional)

Your studies are used to improve models only if you turn this on in Settings → Model improvement. It is off until you do, and reading and reporting work the same either way. When on, the service keeps a de-identified record of each analysis: the analysed image (only DICOM images that declare no burned-in text), sampled CT/MRI slices the model read, the AI findings with your accept and dismiss decisions, the AI draft beside your signed report, and dictation phrases that failed or were corrected, with their audio. Patient name, ID, referring physician, contact details, ID-like numbers and full dates are removed from text; only an age band and sex are kept. Your account and studies are replaced by one-way codes.

A second, separate choice, Also share my saved CT and MRI volumes, lets us make de-identified copies of whole volumes you saved: the file header is reduced to geometry, and the copy is named by a one-way code. Voxels are not changed, so text burned into images is not removed and head scans are not defaced; a face could be reconstructed.

These records are kept in the database (Neon) and the volume copies in Google Cloud Storage, Mumbai, for 365 days, and are used only to train and evaluate RadGenis models. Turning either choice off stops new records; records already kept are not deleted automatically. To have them deleted, contact us.

Retention and deletion

Expired sessions, sign-in attempts, recovery codes and abandoned uploads are cleaned up automatically. Studies and accounts are kept until they are deleted. There is not yet a fixed retention period for them. To have your account or studies deleted, ask your workspace administrator or contact us.
